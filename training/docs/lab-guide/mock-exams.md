# 📝 Mock exams

**Extras → Mock exams** has practice exams: one per course chapter (marked **DE lab**) and
certification exams your lab managers add. Take one as often as you like. Each attempt draws a new
set of questions, your answers are saved as you go, and there is no time limit. After you submit you
get your score, your score per domain, and every question with the right answer and its explanation.

## The exam list
**Exams open to you** lists the exams for all members and for your team. **Search exams…** finds one by
name or provider; the buttons filter it: **All**, **DE lab**, **Certifications**, **In progress**.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Exam** | its name; *closed* when your lab managers have closed it |
| **Questions** | how many questions an attempt has |
| **Pass mark** | the score you need, in % |
| **Attempts** · **Passed** | your submitted attempts, and how many of them passed |
| **Best** · **Average** · **Last** | your scores over those attempts |

**History (n)** opens your past attempts (when, score, result, time) with a **Review** button each, and
**🔁 Questions you keep getting wrong**: up to 20 questions you missed 2 or more times (wrong, partly
right or not answered). Click one to see it in the review.

## Take an attempt
1. Press **Start** (or **New attempt** after your first one). An attempt in progress shows
   **Resume 12/60** instead: it opens where you left it.
2. Answer one question at a time. *choose 2* under the question number means tick two answers.
   Hotspot and drag-and-drop questions have a dropdown per box; yes / no questions have Yes / No per
   statement.
3. Move with **← Previous** / **Next →**, or click a number under **Questions**.
4. **⚐ Mark for review** is a reminder for you to come back to a question. It doesn't change the score.
5. **⏸ Pause** takes you back to the list. Your answers are already saved.
6. **Submit exam** asks first (inline, **Yes, submit** / **Cancel**) and says how many questions are
   not answered or marked for review.

New attempts prefer the questions you have seen least, so a few attempts cover the whole pool.

## How it's scored
- A question with options is right only with **exactly** the right answers. Picking one of two right
  answers is wrong.
- A hotspot, drag-and-drop or yes / no question counts in parts: each box or statement you get right is
  a share of the question's point (*partly right*).
- A question you didn't answer counts as wrong.
- **Score** = points ÷ questions, in %. You pass when the score reaches the exam's pass mark.
- The results per domain add up sub-sections under their section (1.1 and 1.2 under 1).

## The review
At the top: the score, **Passed** or **Not passed yet**, *n of N right*, and a bar per domain. Under
**Questions — click one to see it**, the numbers are coloured: right, partly right, wrong, not answered.

- A right question shows its answer and the explanation.
- A wrong one hides the answer first: pick again and press **Check** (this try isn't marked, your score
  stays), or **Reveal answer**.
- **Reveal all answers** opens every question.
- **New attempt** starts again; **← All exams** goes back to the list.

## When a manager changes an exam
- **Closed**: you can't start it. An attempt in progress opens read-only; submit it as it is to see your
  result. Your past results stay.
- **Attempt reset**: the page says *Your lab managers reset this attempt*. Start the exam again for a new
  set of questions.
- An exam that is no longer open to you (or deleted) leaves the list.
